Player support

Let’s get you back to the table.

Email support@4ohi.com for help. We read every report, but we cannot promise an immediate response.

Before you write

  • Close and reopen the game.
  • Confirm your device has a stable internet connection for online play.
  • Check that the game and operating system are up to date.
  • If a match is interrupted, try the in-game reconnect option before starting over.

Helpful details

Please include:

  • game name and version;
  • device model;
  • operating system and version;
  • what you expected to happen;
  • what happened instead;
  • steps that reproduce the issue;
  • the approximate time of an online-play problem, including your time zone;
  • a screenshot, if it does not expose private information.

Common categories

Game rules or play

Tell us the game, the relevant cards or score, and what looked incorrect.

Connection trouble

Share whether you were joining, reconnecting, or already playing when the issue began.

Display or controls

Include the device orientation and accessibility settings that may affect the layout.

Privacy
