Player support

Let’s get you back to the table.

If something went wrong while testing Palace, Hearts, Spades, or Euchre, send us the details. A clear report helps us understand the problem and improve the game.

Contact support

Email our team

Use one message per issue and include the game name in the subject line.

support@4ohi.com

Do not send passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or unrelated personal information.

What to include

  • Game name and app version
  • Device model
  • Operating-system name and version
  • Steps that reproduce the issue
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Approximate time and time zone for an online-play issue
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it does not reveal private information

Choose the closest category

Installation

Installing, opening, updating, or launching the test build.

Gameplay

Rules, cards, turns, controls, or an unexpected game state.

Ranked Play

Match results, ratings, standings, or ranked-game behavior.

Reconnect

Returning to an interrupted online table or restoring a seat.

Scoring

Points, bids, tricks, rounds, or final results that look incorrect.

Visual or accessibility issue

Text, contrast, focus, motion, orientation, controls, or screen-reader behavior.

Other

Anything that does not fit the categories above.

Try these first

  • Close and reopen the game.
  • Confirm your internet connection is stable for online play.
  • Check that the game and operating system are up to date.
  • If a match was interrupted, try the in-game reconnect option before starting over.
