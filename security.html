Responsible disclosure

Help us keep the table safe.

If you believe you found a security vulnerability in a Four of Hearts game, service, or website, please tell us privately and test responsibly.

Report privately: Email support@4ohi.com with “Security report” in the subject line.

What to include

  • the affected game, service, URL, and version;
  • a clear description of the issue and its potential impact;
  • reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
  • relevant dates, device details, and screenshots;
  • a safe way to contact you.

Please act responsibly

  • Avoid accessing, changing, retaining, or sharing data that is not yours.
  • Do not disrupt services, degrade availability, or test with denial-of-service, spam, or social engineering.
  • Use the minimum testing necessary to demonstrate the concern.
  • Give us reasonable time to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.
  • Do not include live credentials, private keys, or unrelated personal information in your initial report.

What to expect

We will make a reasonable effort to review useful reports and communicate when appropriate. Four of Hearts Interactive does not currently operate a bug-bounty program and does not promise payment or another reward.

For ordinary gameplay issues, please use our Support guide. Our Privacy Policy explains current information practices.
