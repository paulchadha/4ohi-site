Welcome to Four of Hearts Interactive

Meet the South Dakota studio building seven distinct game worlds across card, arcade, platform, puzzle, and adventure genres.

Seven worlds. One studio.

Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC is a South Dakota software company creating original card, arcade, platform, puzzle, and adventure games. The current portfolio spans Palace, Bobby the Breadasaurus, Evil Doom Adventures: Shadow Run, Thumb Command, Hearts, Spades, and Euchre.

We care about games that are easy to begin, rewarding to revisit, and worth sharing across generations. Current products are in development or internal testing, so this site focuses on the work and honest status rather than download promises.

A public home for the work

This newsroom will document meaningful milestones, teaching decisions, safety work, and product stories. We will not manufacture launch dates, player counts, reviews, or partnerships.

If you are helping test a Four of Hearts game, the Support page explains how to send a useful report to support@4ohi.com.
