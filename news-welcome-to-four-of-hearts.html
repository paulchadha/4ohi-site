Welcome to Four of Hearts Interactive

Meet the South Dakota studio building Palace and a growing family of timeless card-table experiences.

One family. Many games.

Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC is a South Dakota software company creating welcoming digital card games. Palace is our flagship, and Hearts, Spades, and Euchre are growing beside it.

We care about games that are easy to begin, rewarding to revisit, and worth sharing across generations. The current family is in Internal Alpha, so this site focuses on the work, the ideas, and honest development updates rather than download promises.

A public home for the work

This newsroom will document meaningful milestones, teaching decisions, safety work, and product stories. We will not manufacture launch dates, player counts, reviews, or partnerships.

If you are helping test a Four of Hearts game, the Support page explains how to send a useful report to support@4ohi.com.

Development status

Palace and every current Four of Hearts game remain in Internal Alpha. This article does not announce public availability.
