Evil Doom Adventures: Shadow Run Enters Development

Two shadow heroes begin a dangerous rescue story built around movement, trust, and finding a way through together.

Two shadows. One rescue.

Evil Doom Adventures: Shadow Run is a new action-platforming world in concept development at Four of Hearts Interactive. Its story follows two shadow heroes through a rescue mission that keeps testing whether they can trust each other.

Current visual and movement explorations include running, jumping, ducking, sliding, climbing, ropes, and swings. These are design directions rather than a claim that a public build exists.

A distinct visual language

Evil Doom Boy moves through black, charcoal, and dangerous red. Evil Doom Girl's approved website identity uses deep purple, anchored by #4E2A84, to stay fierce and distinct without leaning on hot pink.

No release date, public platform, level count, rating, or public availability has been announced.

Availability

Evil Doom Adventures: Shadow Run is in concept development and is not publicly playable. No release date or platform has been announced.
