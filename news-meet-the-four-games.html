Meet the Ten Games in the 4OH Studio

Ten distinct products now share one independent studio, from Palace and Commander Thum-B to puzzle, creative, card-table, and action worlds.

Ten worlds. One studio.

Palace is the playable card-table preview. Commander Thum-B opens a worldwide arcade-defense campaign. Bobby brings tower defence; Evil Doom Adventures brings a dual-hero rescue; HeartStack, Princess Land, and Unicorn Land build distinct puzzle and creative worlds.

Hearts, Spades, and Euchre complete the current portfolio with three kinds of classic card-table strategy.

Still in testing

Every title is still being built or tested. Website lessons demonstrate real ideas from the card games, but they are not complete public releases. Bobby, Evil Doom Adventures, and Commander Thum-B do not have public builds or announced release dates.

More games can join this structure later. Four of Hearts will introduce them only when there is an approved product and an honest status to share.

Availability

This article reports current company work and does not announce public availability.
