Distinctly Palace

Control gives way to mystery.

You begin with cards in hand, move to a visible reserve, and finish with cards no one has seen—not even you. The goal is to get rid of every card before the table catches you.

Palace FAQ

Before you take a seat.

Is the full Palace game publicly available?

Not yet. Palace is in Internal Alpha. This website offers a self-contained teaching preview, not a production multiplayer match or store download.

Why are exact special-card effects not listed?

Palace has many household variants. The Four of Hearts effects are still awaiting final founder approval, so the public site teaches special-card awareness without inventing a rule.

Does the tutorial save anything?

No. It uses page memory only: no account, backend, cookies, analytics, local storage, or saved progress.

Where can I follow development?

The Newsroom publishes honest Internal Alpha updates, and Support explains how invited testers can report issues.
