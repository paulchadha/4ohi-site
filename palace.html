HOW IT PLAYS

A few rules. A table full of possibilities.

01

Match or beat

Play the same rank or higher. Matching cards can go together; if you cannot play, pick up the pile.

02

Keep a power card

A two resets, a seven asks for seven or lower, an eight is transparent, and a ten burns the pile.

03

Reach the hidden finish

Empty your hand, then your face-up reserve. Your final face-down cards turn a careful plan into a surprise.

THE PEOPLE AT THE TABLE

Pull up a chair.

The app offers one to three bots or automatic seating at an online ranked table. The tested online flow handles joining, readying, and play without a room-code hunt. Private invitations and community features are still ahead.

WHAT CARRIES FORWARD

The score has a story.

The app keeps separate People and Bots ratings on the device. Online games can reconnect during an active session. A lasting cloud ranking and a career shared across devices are still ahead. The website saves one local bot game in this browser.

Current availability

App in private testing. Online play has passed focused phone-and-browser checks; recovery from interrupted service is still being improved. The complete website game below is local play against bots.
