Building a Safer, Family-Friendly Card Table

How Four of Hearts approaches clear policies, restrained data use, accessibility, and honest Internal Alpha communication.

Trust starts with plain language

A family-friendly game company should explain what is available, what is being tested, and what information may be involved. Our public pages distinguish the static website from Internal Alpha game services and avoid promises the current product cannot support.

The website has no analytics, advertising, account system, form backend, cookie, tracking pixel, or social widget. Its tutorials keep progress only in page memory.

Safety is ongoing work

Accessibility, security, privacy, reconnect behavior, rankings, operational logs, and support processes all require review as the games evolve. Our policies describe current limitations and identify the questions that must be revisited before a wider release.

Security reports and player-support requests currently use support@4ohi.com. We do not promise a bug bounty or a guaranteed response time.

Availability

This article reports current company work and does not announce public availability.
