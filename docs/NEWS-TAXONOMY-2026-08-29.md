# News taxonomy and status rules

The News page exposes **All**, one filter for each of the ten products, and **Studio**. Cards derive tags from category, product key, and story tags. Empty states remain visible and accessible.

Five August 29 development dispatches add current coverage for Bobby tower defence, Evil Doom dual heroes, HeartStack, Princess Land, and Unicorn Land. Thumb Command stories retain their existing technical slugs for compatibility while displaying the approved title. A story may say released only when a working public release exists; none of the seven in-development Original Worlds is presented as released.
