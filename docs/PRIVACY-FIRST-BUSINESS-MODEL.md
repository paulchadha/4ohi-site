# Privacy-First Business Model

Reviewed: August 30, 2026

## Position

Four of Hearts sells software and does not monetize attention, behavioral profiles, or personal information. The preferred model is a straightforward software purchase. A modest recurring subscription is considered only when a product supplies real continuing value such as ongoing content, meaningful updates, multiplayer/service operation, or cloud support. Exact price, billing frequency, renewal, benefits, and cancellation terms must appear clearly before purchase.

## Current official platform review

- Apple App Review Guidelines: auto-renewable subscriptions must provide ongoing value and clearly describe what the user receives. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- Apple Paid Apps Agreement: the Account Holder must accept the Paid Apps Agreement to sell paid apps or in-app purchases. https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-agreements/sign-and-update-agreements/
- Apple App Privacy: declarations must cover first-party and third-party SDK practices and stay accurate; data processed only on-device is treated differently from collected data. https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Google Play Subscriptions: subscriptions must provide sustained or recurring value and disclose cost, billing frequency, renewal, whether required, benefits, and cancellation. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9900533
- Google Play Data safety: developers are responsible for accurate declarations, including third-party SDK behavior, and a privacy policy is required even when an app collects no user data. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469

## Release gate

No product may inherit the website's privacy status. Before distribution, record the build identifier, data flow, SDK inventory, accounts, cloud services, multiplayer, advertising, analytics, crash reporting, retention, deletion, age design, and store disclosure answers in the product privacy profile. “Not yet verified” is the correct public label until that work is complete.
