# Studio portfolio artwork provenance

Founder-supplied source boards are preserved under `docs/source-assets/founder-studio-portfolio-2026-08-29/`. Responsive derivatives are generated by `scripts/process-founder-portfolio-art.py` into `assets/portfolio-2026/`.

- `bobby-system.png` and `bobby-gameplay.png` → Bobby tower-defense hero/gameplay derivatives.
- `commander-cities.png` → Thumb Command worldwide campaign derivative.
- `heartstack.png` → HeartStack Unicorn Blast derivative.
- `lands-a.png` and `lands-b.png` → separate Princess Land and Unicorn Land derivatives.
- Evil Doom uses the previously approved deep-purple derivatives in `assets/evil-doom/`.
- Palace and the three classic card games retain their approved 4OH artwork.

Functional titles, status, actions, and descriptions are HTML. Text baked into founder boards is illustrative and is not authoritative product metadata. No global colour filter is applied to artwork.
