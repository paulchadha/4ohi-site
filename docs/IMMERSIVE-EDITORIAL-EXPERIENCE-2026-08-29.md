# Immersive editorial experience — August 29, 2026

## Decision

The current production visual authority is the Palace-first immersive editorial layer in `assets/playable-studio.css`, generated by `scripts/homepage-playable-studio.mjs` and progressively enhanced by `assets/studio-reconstruction.js`. Earlier reconstruction notes remain historical evidence, not current layout authority.

The official Accenture Song site was used only as a quality benchmark for pacing: cinematic openings, confident typography, generous transitions, varied editorial composition, and work presented as distinct worlds. Four of Hearts uses original structure, copy, interactions, and approved local artwork.

## Homepage and hierarchy

Four of Hearts Interactive remains the parent company and 4OH the compact mark. Palace deliberately dominates the homepage campaign because it is the flagship and the only featured playable mini-match. Bobby the Breadasaurus, Evil Doom Adventures: Shadow Run, Commander Thum-B, Hearts, Spades, and Euchre follow as seven distinct portfolio worlds sourced from `scripts/game-catalog.mjs`.

The page sequence is: Palace campaign hero; one honest October 17, 2026 countdown; mini-match action; Match/Beat/Burn/Win teaching scene; 2/7/8/10 power-card river; responsible folk-history transition; four-story News edit; seven-world portfolio; Four daughters/Four hearts family story; founder signoff.

## Visual system

- Deep midnight and royal blue establish the studio and game-table atmosphere.
- Warm ivory editorial scenes improve long-form readability.
- Gold is reserved for action, focus, countdown, and Palace authority.
- Approved game art is displayed at cinematic scale with contrast overlays rather than decorative floating objects.
- Layouts alternate full-bleed, split, asymmetrical, and river compositions; repeated card grids are not the default.
- Typography uses local system stacks only. No remote font request is allowed.

## Motion and interaction

`assets/studio-reconstruction.js` adds scroll-aware header state and viewport reveal classes. All content is present without JavaScript, all links remain native, and `prefers-reduced-motion` disables nonessential transitions. The script uses no cookies, local storage, session storage, analytics, or tracking.

## Responsive contract

The composition is authored mobile-first, including 320, 375, and 430 CSS-pixel widths, portrait and landscape tablets, laptop, and large desktop. Mobile collapses decorative layers, preserves artwork focal points, keeps targets at least 44 CSS pixels, and never requires horizontal scrolling. Palace gameplay remains a separate usable route rather than being squeezed into the campaign hero.

## News and portfolio authoring

News is ordered with the featured story first and uses one lead feature plus an editorial stack. Game entries come only from `scripts/game-catalog.mjs`; a future game must supply a canonical title, honest status, artwork, description, and destination before it can enter the portfolio.

## Accessibility, privacy, and performance

The generated pages retain a skip link, one H1, semantic landmarks, visible focus, current-page indication, keyboard/mobile navigation, meaningful image alternatives, reduced-motion support, and 44-pixel targets. The static validation gate rejects remote assets, tracking, storage, mixed content, broken internal links, and missing metadata.

Local release measurements for the homepage:

- 375×812: 25 requests, 838,400 declared bytes, no third-party hosts, no overflow, DOM content loaded about 221 ms.
- 1440×900: 25 requests, 903,466 declared bytes, no third-party hosts, no overflow, DOM content loaded about 217 ms and load about 338 ms.

These are local diagnostic timings, not promises about a visitor's network.

## Build, evidence, and deployment

Run `node scripts/build-site.mjs`, `node scripts/validate-site.mjs`, the company/portfolio/Palace/founder/reconstruction browser suites, then `git diff --check`. Current screenshots and JSON results are written under `docs/visual-evidence/reconstruction-2026-08-29/` and the other canonical evidence directories.

Publish only by pushing a reviewed commit to `main`, waiting for GitHub Pages to report success for that exact SHA, and rerunning the public-site validator against `https://4ohi.com`. Roll back with a normal revert commit; never rewrite public history or alter DNS/email records for a visual release.

Founder physical visual acceptance remains the final subjective gate.
