# Brand assets

## Brand system

Four of Hearts uses warm cream surfaces, deep forest green, restrained heart red, and small gold accents. Typography pairs a system sans-serif stack with Georgia for friendly editorial character. Card corners, tactile paper, and suit details make the identity recognizable without casino or gambling cues.

Use the full company name “Four of Hearts Interactive” in primary identity contexts. Public copy must remain family-friendly and must not imply store availability before a verified release.

## Game icons

The four web icons were copied from the company-controlled PalaceApp repository on 2026-07-25 and resized to 512×512 without changing their artwork:

- `assets/icon-palace-4hearts.png`
- `assets/icon-hearts-4hearts.png`
- `assets/icon-spades-4hearts.png`
- `assets/icon-euchre-4hearts.png`

PalaceApp was not modified during this website sprint. Treat the icons as company branding based on their controlled source, but confirm final trademark and artwork provenance before public commercial launch. Do not stretch, recolor, crop, or overlay them.

High-quality .webp delivery derivatives with matching base names are used by the website. The source PNGs remain the preservation copies. The WebP files reduce transferred game-art weight by about 90% without changing dimensions or composition.

`assets/favicon.png` is a 64×64 derivative of the Hearts icon. The site’s heart mark and playing-card treatments are text and CSS, not separate logo files.

## Social preview

## Store metadata

Before using any asset in Apple or Google listings, confirm required dimensions, transparency, screenshots, ownership, age rating, privacy disclosures, support URL, privacy URL, and exact product name. Store copy must match live app behavior and actual public availability.

## Approval dependency

A formally approved standalone company logo and recorded source-art license are not present in this repository. Replace or augment the text/CSS identity when approved source material becomes available.
