# Brand assets

## Source and ownership

The four public game icons were copied from the company-controlled PalaceApp repository on 2026-07-25 and resized to 512×512 for web delivery without changing their artwork:

- `assets/icon-palace-4hearts.png`
- `assets/icon-hearts-4hearts.png`
- `assets/icon-spades-4hearts.png`
- `assets/icon-euchre-4hearts.png`

Source paths were `PalaceApp/assets/<filename>`. PalaceApp itself was not modified. These assets are treated as company-owned Four of Hearts game branding based on their repository location and active app configuration. Confirm final trademark and art provenance before public commercial launch.

`assets/favicon.png` is a 64×64 derivative of the Hearts icon for browser-icon use. The website's simple heart mark is rendered as text and CSS; there is no separate logo file.

## Usage

- Do not stretch, recolor, crop, or place overlays on game icons.
- Preserve clear space around icons.
- Use the full company name “Four of Hearts Interactive” in primary identity contexts.
- Use a warm cream background, deep green, restrained red, and gold accents consistent with the website stylesheet.
- Do not imply store availability before a verified release.

## Apple and Google metadata

Before using these assets in store listings, confirm required pixel sizes, transparency rules, screenshots, ownership, age rating, privacy disclosures, support URL, privacy URL, and the exact product name. Store copy must match the live app behavior and public availability.

## Review dependency

A formally approved company logo and documented source-art license were not available in the audited repository. Replace or augment the text/CSS identity when a final approved logo is supplied.
