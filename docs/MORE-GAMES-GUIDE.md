# More Games page guide

Palace/Shed is the flagship. Hearts, Spades, and Euchre are the supporting family, all still labeled Internal Alpha.

## First viewport

On a 1440×900 desktop viewport, the page must show:

- the global logo, navigation, table-name control, language control, and Settings;
- the slim release countdown;
- the compact "More ways to own the table" heading;
- visible artwork and calls to action for Hearts, Spades, and Euchre.

Do not restore a full-screen title card above the game shelf.

## Game cards

Use the approved founder-binder artwork. Keep each card's promise short:

- Hearts: dodge the points or take every one;
- Spades: bid together and use trump;
- Euchre: call trump and find the bowers.

Every call to action enters the existing two-decision Quick Play lesson. Do not imply a complete public game or public store availability.

## Flagship return

The lower Palace band returns visitors to the three-layer mini-match. Its visible name must follow the selected URL state; canonical image alternatives and SEO identity may remain Palace.

## Acceptance

Verify 320, 390, 768, 1366, 1440, and 1920 widths, keyboard focus, readable game-card order in RTL, no horizontal overflow, no stored state, and all three tutorial completion/replay paths.


## Table differentiation

Hearts, Spades, and Euchre now expose their own score/state vocabulary, rules, accents, and table decisions inside the shared app-world frame. Recoloring one generic tutorial is not acceptable.


## Future-table presentation

The future section uses a blue-purple-ruby mystery-card treatment and does not name unapproved games. Product cards retain equal heights, consistent crops, aligned actions, and game-specific feedback.

## Seven-world portfolio update — 2026-08-29

The homepage portfolio is generated from `scripts/game-catalog.mjs` and presents all ten games as distinct full-width worlds. Add future games to the catalog with approved local artwork, honest status, description, and canonical link; do not hand-code a homepage-only entry.
