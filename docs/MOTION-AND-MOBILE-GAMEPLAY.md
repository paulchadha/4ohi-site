# Motion and Mobile Gameplay Standard

Use motion only to clarify deal, selection, play, pickup, burn, trick collection, reflow, reveal, bid/trump confirmation, and results. Tap response is immediate; animation never blocks input longer than the guided transition and never duplicates a card. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables transitions and animations.

Phone layouts are composed, not scaled. At 320–430 pixels Power Cards use two columns, Palace uses stacked opponent/setup/pile/hand regions, secondary games stack center and decision regions, controls remain at least 44 pixels, and no horizontal scrolling is permitted. Tablet verification includes 768×1024. Landscape may improve the table but portrait remains usable.
## Immersive motion gate — 2026-07-31

Scene reveals, spatial gallery transitions, and page arrival are enhancement-only. Reduced-motion mode removes them. The mobile opening was tested at 320×568, 360×800, 390×844, 412×915, and 430×932; tablet at 768×1024; desktop at 1366×768 and 1920×1080. The Palace controls use a two-row mobile utility layout and all tested visible targets meet the 44px gate.
