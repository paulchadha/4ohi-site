# Brand Voice and Canadian Mode

## Default voice

Four of Hearts speaks like a confident family card-table host: clear, warm, mischievous, and never corporate for its own sake. The game may be legendary; the studio should still sound human.

## Canadian

The selector label is exactly **Canadian**. It is a playful language/voice mode, not a legal or geographic claim. It changes multiple parts of the site: Table Talk, Our Family, Help Please, hero copy, news, game-family copy, support, 404, footer, countdown, and tutorial prompts. A single changed button is not sufficient QA.

Use Canadian jokes lightly: table manners, weather, long winters, snacks, “eh,” “bud,” and apologizing while burning the pile. Do not stereotype people, imitate accents phonetically, or turn every sentence into a joke.

## Product names

- **Palace**: canonical public, SEO, metadata, and structured-data name.
- **Shed**: selectable family-friendly table name.
- **Shithead**: optional traditional name behind explicit adult confirmation; URL-only and never stored.

Historical prose may explain that the folk game travels under several names. Dynamic product UI must follow the current `This Table Calls It` selection.
