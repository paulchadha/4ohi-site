# SEO keyword and content governance

## Search intent

The site should answer real player questions, not repeat keywords. Priority intent families are:

- Palace card game rules;
- how to play Palace;
- Palace power cards and the 2, 7, 8, and 10;
- Palace strategy;
- Shed card game and shedding games;
- Palace alternate names and house rules;
- family and multiplayer card games;
- the coming Four of Hearts Palace app.

`palace-faq.html` is the primary rules-and-FAQ answer page. `palace-play.html` teaches by interaction. `palace-story.html` separates documented information, table tradition, and founder-supplied legend. News documents product progress.

## Canonical naming

Palace remains the title, metadata, Open Graph, RSS, sitemap, and structured-data identity. Shed may appear in useful visible explanatory copy. The private traditional adult name is excluded from discovery metadata.

No alternate-name doorway pages are allowed. Locale query parameters use the same canonical URL and approved hreflang set.

## Evidence and claims

The Pagat rules/history page is the primary external reference for published alternate names, common gameplay structure, special-card variation, and the documented uncertainty around origin:

`https://www.pagat.com/beating/shithead.html`

Do not claim verified player counts, rankings, partnerships, reviews, geography dominance, launch success, or exact origin without a retained primary source. "Hottest card game in the galaxy" is obvious campaign hyperbole, not a measurable claim.

## Content quality

Every discovery page must:

1. answer a distinct question;
2. link naturally to the mini-match, full rules/FAQ, history, or current News;
3. distinguish Four of Hearts rules from all traditional house rules;
4. avoid keyword stuffing and unsupported superlatives presented as fact;
5. preserve readable headings, descriptive link text, and meaningful image alternatives;
6. keep Palace canonical in metadata even when visible URL state says Shed.

## Release checks

Run the generator twice, static validation, app-parity browser QA, and a metadata/structured-data inspection. Confirm sitemap and RSS parse, canonical URLs are HTTPS, the FAQ JSON-LD is valid JSON, and no private display name entered discovery surfaces.
