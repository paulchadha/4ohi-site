# Asset Provenance

Four of Hearts Interactive treats repository art as proprietary production material. No third-party stock library is introduced by this release.

- `assets/card-games/solitaire-tile.svg` and `.png/.webp` derivatives: original in-house code-authored card art, created 2026-08-30, owner Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC; commercial use approved.
- `assets/card-games/war-tile.svg` and `.png/.webp` derivatives: original in-house code-authored card art, created 2026-08-30, owner Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC; commercial use approved.
- `assets/portfolio-2026/evil-doom-card.svg/.png`: one unified, non-destructive two-hero composition derived from founder-approved Evil Doom artwork; Evil Doom Boy is the canonical product title and default hero, Evil Doom Girl is the alternate hero, and the production DOM adds the restrained “Choose Your Hero” label. `assets/evil-doom/evil-doom-boy-preview.png` and `evil-doom-girl-preview.png` are title-free selector crops derived from the approved `evil-doom-hero-purple-1200.webp`. Owner Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC; commercial use approved for this site.
- `assets/portfolio-2026/princess-land-card.svg/.png` and `unicorn-land-card.svg/.png`: focused non-destructive compositions derived from the same founder-approved board; owner Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC; commercial use approved for this site.
- `assets/lifestyle/people-lens.svg` and `sleep-amigo.svg`: original in-house code-authored concept art, created 2026-08-30; owner Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC; commercial use approved for this site.
- Existing Palace, Hearts, Spades, Euchre, Thumb Command, Bobby, Evil Doom, HeartStack, Princess Land, and Unicorn Land assets were already in the production repository as founder-provided/approved project art. Their use remains limited to Four of Hearts projects.

The machine-readable companion is `docs/asset-provenance.json`. Source boards remain documentation/source material; focused derivatives—not full labelled concept sheets—are used as catalogue art.
