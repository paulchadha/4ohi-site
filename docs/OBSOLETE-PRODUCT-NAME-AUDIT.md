# Obsolete product-name audit

Date: 2026-08-29

## Canonical names

- Thumb Command
- Evil Doom Girl Adventures

Evil Doom Boy remains a playable hero inside Evil Doom Girl Adventures. He is not a separate product.

## Corrected surfaces

Active templates, product data, metadata, JSON-LD, navigation, Canadian copy, news content, generated HTML, RSS, sitemap, accessibility text, and verification scripts now use the canonical names.

## Compatibility routes retained

The former Commander and Shadow Run URLs remain only as `noindex,follow` redirects. They are not canonical, do not appear in the sitemap, and exist solely to avoid breaking old public links.

## Historical records

Older evidence and launch records may quote previous names as historical facts. They are non-production artifacts and must not be copied into current marketing or metadata.
