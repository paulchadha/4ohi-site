# Four of Hearts Studio Voice

Reviewed: August 30, 2026

## Core position

Four of Hearts Interactive is an independent South Dakota software company. It makes games and productivity/lifestyle applications, and it can build focused custom applications quickly.

**Core line:** GAMES WITH HEART. APPS WITH PURPOSE.

The voice is independent, clever, warm, technically serious, privacy-minded, slightly weird, confident, playful, and handcrafted. It never sounds like a growth funnel or a generic “new worlds” portfolio.

## Writing rules

- Say what the software does before adding personality.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs to “experiences,” “journeys,” “solutions,” and generic “worlds.”
- Use short sentences, specific premises, and product verbs.
- Let one strong idea own each section. Do not stack slogans.
- Be honest about development status, pricing, architecture, and uncertainty.
- Explain the craft through careful engineering, thoughtful design, physical-device testing, accessibility, iteration, documentation, and privacy by design.
- Keep the origin simple: four kids, a house full of games, and a company that came later.
- State the point of view when it matters: independent companies should still be allowed to make wonderfully strange things.
- Humor is seasoning, never camouflage.

## Product cadence

- Thumb Command is urgent and cinematic.
- Bobby the Breadasaurus is tactically absurd.
- Evil Doom Adventures is brisk, dark, and mischievous.
- HeartStack is sparkling and kinetic.
- Princess Land and Unicorn Land are inviting and creative, but distinct from each other.
- Card games are concise, familiar, and table-specific.
- People Lens is direct and evidence-minded.
- Sleep Amigo is calm, personal, and clear.

## Privacy language

The conceptual center is: **Personal information should remain personal.**

Approved positioning includes “We love data. We just do not think all of yours belongs to us” and “We make apps, not dossiers.” Claims must remain scoped to verified website behavior or clearly qualified product plans. Never claim that an unreleased app is private merely because this website is lean.

## Canadian mode

Canadian copy may be warm, dry, and lightly regional. It may add a toque, prairie weather, or an understated “bud.” Formal legal policy, prices, release status, safety information, and privacy/consent controls stay literal.
