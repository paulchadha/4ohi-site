# Four of Hearts Voice

Four of Hearts sounds independent, specific, useful, and lightly amused. It does not sound like a growth funnel.

- Say what the software does before adding personality.
- Prefer concrete nouns and verbs to “experiences,” “journeys,” and generic “worlds.”
- Be honest about development status, pricing, architecture, and uncertainty.
- Let each product keep its own cadence: Thumb Command is urgent; Bobby is tactically absurd; Evil Doom Girl is brisk and dangerous; HeartStack is sparkling and kinetic; the creative titles are inviting; card games are concise.
- Canadian copy may be warm and dry. Legal disclosures, consent choices, prices, and safety information stay literal.
- Humor is seasoning, never camouflage.
- Never claim privacy for an unreleased app merely because the website is lean.
