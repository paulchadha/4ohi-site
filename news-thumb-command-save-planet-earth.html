Introducing Thumb Command: Save Planet Earth

Meet the Blueguard interceptor, the cities it protects, and the worldwide fight at the heart of Four of Hearts Interactive’s new arcade-defense direction.

A new command for Planet Earth

Thumb Command is a new creative direction from Four of Hearts Interactive: a fast, colorful arcade-defense game built around one clear promise—save Planet Earth.

Alien forces are descending on the cities below. The player launches the blue-white-gold Blueguard interceptor, breaks incoming formations, protects the city shield, and keeps every projectile away from the people beneath the battle.

The city is the base

There is no expendable outpost. Chicago, San Francisco, New York City, London, Tokyo, and future campaign destinations are the bases. Their skylines, weather, shields, and attack patterns give each defense a different pulse.

Thumb Command is in development. No public build, platform release, or release date has been announced; future availability news will be published only when it is real.

Availability
