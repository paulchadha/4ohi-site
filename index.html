THE NAME

Four kids. One house full of games.

The company came later.

The name comes from four kids and a house where something was always being played. What stayed was the important part: make products we want to use, make titles we want to play, and treat the people using both with respect.

Why Four of Hearts ↗

PRIVACY IS PART OF THE PRODUCT

You’re the customer. Not the product.

Four of Hearts makes paid software. Our business is not built around watching what you do or selling what we learn. We collect as little information as a product actually needs, keep data on-device whenever practical, and stay away from behavioral advertising and cross-app profiling.
