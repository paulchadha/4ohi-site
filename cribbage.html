THE GAME AT THE TABLE

Keep four.
Try to look as though that was the plan.

Cribbage brings its walnut board, ruby and sapphire pegs, and familiar card-table decisions into the Palace family. The private app deals a real hand and lets you choose what to keep. The computer does not accept an apologetic explanation with your discards.

What is ready to show

Private Android 0.2.0 build 2 is installed on our Samsung tablet. Local practice is under hands-on testing. No public store release, download, live human table or verified online ranking is announced.

These are previews of a private test build. The screenshots are images, not playable controls.

The important bits stay in view.

Make the discard

Choose two cards from six for the crib. Our Samsung tablet test exercised the actual selection and confirmation controls.

Play the count

Local Standard practice follows the familiar race to 121, with a running count and automatic scoring. We are testing the complete rhythm, not declaring every edge case finished.

Take your time

The current home offers Easy and Standard computer choices. Practice stays unrated; online tables and independent-account acceptance remain separate work.
