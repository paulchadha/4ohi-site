Bobby the Breadasaurus Builds a Tower-Defense World

Bobby’s current direction puts the little dinosaur at the centre of a colourful, bread-powered tower-defense adventure.

A small hero holds a very busy path

Four of Hearts Interactive is developing Bobby the Breadasaurus: Tower Defense Adventures, a bright strategy game about placing bread-powered towers, reading incoming routes, and protecting the Breadstone.

The project is in internal development. Current artwork and systems establish direction, not a promise about final platforms, features, or release timing.

Build, upgrade, and move Bobby

Toast Zappers, Knife Blocks, Crumb Blasters, and Flour Storm towers make the battlefield readable and playful while Bobby’s own movement gives the player a heroic decision beyond tower placement.

The warmer tower-defense identity replaces the earlier public platform-adventure framing.

Availability

Bobby the Breadasaurus is in concept development and is not publicly playable. No release date or platform has been announced.
