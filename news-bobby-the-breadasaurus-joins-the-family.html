Bobby the Breadasaurus Joins the Four of Hearts Family

A brave little dinosaur and a bread-filled world begin their journey at Four of Hearts Interactive.

A small hero enters a very big world

Four of Hearts Interactive is developing Bobby the Breadasaurus, a warm family adventure about a brave little dinosaur, a scattered herd, and a world where bread seems to turn up in the most unlikely places.

The project is in concept development. The artwork on this site establishes a direction, not a promise about final gameplay, platforms, characters, or release timing.

Adventure, friendship, and bread

The design exploration centers on responsive platforming, collecting bread, dodging imaginative dangers, and helping Bobby find the way home.

Bobby brings a sunny new kind of world to the Four of Hearts portfolio while keeping the studio's focus intact: welcome newcomers, reward curiosity, and make exploration feel good.

Availability

Bobby the Breadasaurus is in concept development and is not publicly playable. No release date or platform has been announced.
