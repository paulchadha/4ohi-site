Building Commander ThumB: One Thumb, One Ship, 1,000 Levels

The design promise behind Commander ThumB is immediate control, escalating spectacle, and a long campaign built for mobile play.

One-thumb clarity

The control idea is direct: drag to move, keep contact to fire, and let positioning make the difference. The title’s capital B is part of both the name and the game’s one-thumb identity.

Power-ups, Wing ThumBs, ship improvements, and base defenses add depth without obscuring the immediate action.

Built for a long campaign

The approved design target spans 1,000 levels across ten escalating sectors and boss encounters. That is a development direction, not a claim that a public version is available today.

Commander ThumB remains Coming Soon. Four of Hearts will publish release news only when there is a working public version.

Availability

Commander ThumB is Coming Soon and is not publicly playable. This article does not announce a release.
