Meet the Blueguard: Earth’s Last Interceptor

Inside Thumb Command’s blue-white-gold hero ship, responsive arcade control, and Vanguard, Sentinel, and Paladin upgrade path.

Built for speed and clarity

The Blueguard’s royal blue, white, cyan, and gold silhouette is designed to stay readable against deep space, bright shields, city lights, and dense enemy formations.

Responsive movement keeps the ship connected to the player’s thumb while its weapon rhythm lets positioning remain the main decision. Speed, attack spread, shields, missiles, and repairs deepen the run without burying the arcade action.

Vanguard. Sentinel. Paladin.

Vanguard is the first major refit, sharpening the Blueguard for a faster offensive push. Sentinel reinforces defensive systems for longer waves. Paladin is the ship at full authority: broader, brighter, and equipped for the largest boss formations.

Purple and magenta alien technology creates an instant visual contrast. Even at the most crowded moment, the player should know which ship is theirs and where safety lies.

Availability

Thumb Command is in development with no public build or announced release date. This article documents the current creative direction.
