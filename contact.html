How can we help?

One address, a clear subject.

Write to support@4ohi.com and use a subject that identifies your topic:

  • Game support: include the game name and app version.
  • Privacy request: begin the subject with “Privacy request.”
  • Security report: begin the subject with “Security report” and follow our responsible disclosure guidance.
  • General inquiry: briefly describe why you are writing.

We do not publish a home address or personal telephone number.
