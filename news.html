ALSO ON THE WORKBENCH · IN DEVELOPMENT

Press Ahead

A separate expedition-inspired match-three game following the Lewis and Clark journey, built from the Unicorn Blast foundation. Unicorn Blast remains its own game.

“Press a head” and move ahead. A little wordplay for a long journey. We are trying not to let it go to our heads.

Press Ahead is still in development, with no public release date announced. A little head start and a well-packed lunch seem sensible.
