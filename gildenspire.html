THE SKY IS ONLY THE BEGINNING

Fly farther.
Fight harder.
Grow legendary.

GildenSpire is being built around the thrill of direct flight: climbing, diving, banking, exploring, discovering—and fighting when the open sky stops being friendly.

01 · RAISE

Raise a Legend

Every GildenSpire dragon begins small. Hatch it, feed it, train it, build a bond with it, and watch it develop into something much more powerful.

Different dragons are being designed with genuinely different shapes, powers, movement characteristics, strengths, and personalities.

02 · FLY

The World Is Bigger From Up Here

Flying is not transportation between menus. Flying is part of the game.

Take direct control and cross cliffs, islands, castles, ruins, secrets, weather, and places that can only be reached from the sky.

03 · FIGHT

Own the Sky

Exploration becomes flight-or-fight when something else claims the air.

Dive. Climb. Bank. Dodge. Chase. Attack.

Different dragons will bring different abilities to kinetic aerial battles, with team play planned for larger threats.

DIFFERENT DRAGONS. DIFFERENT POWERS.

Dragons of GildenSpire

The world of GildenSpire is being built around dragons that are more than color swaps. Different silhouettes. Different movement. Different powers. Different ways to fly and fight.

These species define the current creative direction; development and implementation will continue to evolve.

  • EmberclawFirepower with weight behind it.
  • FrostveilCold air. Clean lines. A different fight.
  • VerdantisPower drawn from the living world.
  • TidalisBuilt for coastlines, storms, and open water.
  • SkysongLight, quick, and at home above the clouds.
  • StormcrestThunder made airborne.
  • DuneheartEndurance shaped by heat and distance.
  • ShadowmireLow light, quiet wings, patient power.
  • ObsidianArmoured, imposing, and hard to move.
  • DawnplumeA bright silhouette against the morning sky.
  • MoonlaceGraceful flight with an unusual rhythm.
  • AurumThe legend beyond the Golden Egg.
