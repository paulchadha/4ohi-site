From the workshop

The name going forward is Unicorn Blast.

The development direction is brighter, smoother, and easier to understand: stronger home and campaign screens, satisfying effects, and tools that explain what they do before you have to guess.

Bombs and boosters should be fun because you understand the opportunity—not because you accidentally discover what that sparkling object was for.

We’re chasing the moment when a good move becomes a glorious chain reaction.

The unicorns deserve a rescue with a little production value.

Current availability

Unicorn Blast: In development; no public release date or store availability announced.
