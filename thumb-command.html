The mission

The City Is the Base

Purple and magenta alien fleets are descending over Earth’s great cities. Launch the blue-white-gold Blueguard interceptor, keep the shield alive, and turn every skyline into a last line of defense.

  1. MoveSlip between enemy fire with immediate arcade control.
  2. FireBreak formations, collect energy, and build a clean attack rhythm.
  3. DefendRepair the city, reinforce its shield, and survive the boss wave.
