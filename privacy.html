Privacy Policy

Effective: July 25, 2026 · Applies to: this website and internal-alpha versions of Palace, Hearts, Spades, and Euchre

Four of Hearts Interactive, LLC (“Four of Hearts,” “we,” “us”) makes card games and operates this website. This policy explains the information our current internal-alpha services use and why.

Information used by the games

Current builds may store your chosen player name, avatar, optional profile-photo reference, ratings, game preferences, and a randomly generated device identifier on your device. A reconnect credential may also be stored locally so an interrupted online game can restore your seat.

When you use online or ranked play, the game sends information needed to run the match to our game server. This may include a random player identifier, display name, avatar or profile image, game actions, table and session identifiers, reconnect information, match state, results, and rating data. Other players at your table may see your display name, avatar or profile image, game actions, and appropriate public game state.

Accounts and authentication

Internal-alpha builds do not currently provide a production user-account system. The locally generated identifier used for alpha play is not a verified identity or a secure login.

Rankings and retention

Current alpha multiplayer sessions, tables, and rankings are held in server memory rather than a durable production database. Session and table information expires when it is no longer needed for play and reconnection; in-memory rankings and related records are cleared when the service restarts. Information stored on your device remains until you clear app data, remove it through available settings, or uninstall the game.

Logs and crash information

Our server is designed to log operational health information such as version, uptime, and aggregate active-table counts without logging reconnect credentials, table contents, player identities, or profile data. Current builds do not include a dedicated third-party advertising or behavioral-analytics SDK.

Your device platform, app-store provider, update provider, or hosting provider may process standard technical information—such as IP address, request time, device details, or crash diagnostics—under its own settings and privacy terms. We may receive crash or diagnostic information when you choose to share it with us.

This website

This website does not use advertising, analytics pixels, or browser cookies set by Four of Hearts. Like most hosting services, our host may process basic request information, including IP address, browser type, requested page, and time, to deliver and secure the site.

Support messages

If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, such as your email address, message, device and app details, and attachments. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, protect our services, and maintain appropriate business records. Do not send secrets or unrelated sensitive information.

How we use information

  • provide local and online gameplay;
  • match players, maintain sessions, calculate alpha rankings, and reconnect interrupted games;
  • respond to support, privacy, and security messages;
  • diagnose reliability and security problems;
  • comply with law and protect players, our company, and our services.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may provide limited information to service providers that help operate email, hosting, app distribution, or game infrastructure; when required by law; or when necessary to protect rights, safety, and service integrity. Public player information is shared with participants as described above.

Children and families

Our games are intended to be welcoming to families, but the current online alpha service is not directed to children under 13 and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information should contact privacy@4ohi.com so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to an internal alpha, including minimizing logged information and separating private game state by player seat. No system is perfectly secure. Please report suspected vulnerabilities through our security page.

Your choices and requests

You may change in-game profile details where settings allow, clear local app data, uninstall the game, or contact us about access, correction, or deletion. Because current alpha data is primarily local or short-lived and may not be linked to a verified identity, we may need information that reasonably identifies the relevant session or support message.

Policy updates

We will update this policy as our games, infrastructure, and legal obligations change. We will post the new effective date here and provide additional notice when appropriate.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests: privacy@4ohi.com
