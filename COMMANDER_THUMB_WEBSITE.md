# Commander ThumB Website

## Route and integration

- Production route: `/commander-thumb.html`
- Canonical URL: `https://4ohi.com/commander-thumb.html`
- Generated by `scripts/build-site.mjs` from `scripts/commander-content.mjs`.
- Shared desktop and mobile navigation use the exact label **Commander ThumB** and expose an active state.
- The shared footer links to the route.
- The Palace-led homepage, More Games page, and Quick Play directory include a Coming Soon Commander ThumB tile.
- Commander ThumB is not presented as publicly playable and has no invented release date.

## Approved spelling and lore

- The game is always **Commander ThumB**. The final capital `B` is intentional.
- The star is **Thum**.
- The planets are **Thum A**, **Thum B**, and **Thum C**.
- Thum A invades Thum B.
- Thum B defends itself and is the home of Commander ThumB.
- Thum C is a scientific and educational civilization observing the conflict. It has not joined either side.
- All three worlds are inhabited by related ThumB civilizations.
- The reason for the invasion remains unresolved.
- The campaign contains 1,000 levels and a different major boss every 100 levels.

1. Cinematic hero and Coming Soon mission action.

4. Thum A, Thum B, and Thum C identity key.

8. Defend Thum B gameplay features and Wing ThumBs.
9. Ten approved boss milestones.
10. Unresolved Thum C mystery and final Coming Soon action.

Approved production sources were copied from the separate Commander ThumB game project without modifying that project.

- Optimized 960- and 480-pixel WebP game tiles.
- Optimized transparent horizontal logo.
- Approved Open Graph image.
- Commander ThumB character, Commander-Class ship, shielded base, and emblem.

- Six representative enemy ships.

The original game art remains governed by the Commander ThumB production-art pipeline. The website copies are deployment derivatives only.

## Theme tokens

`assets/commander-thumb.css` defines reusable `--ct-*` tokens:

- cosmic space and navy surfaces;
- electric royal blue and cyan;
- commander gold and solar orange;
- plasma purple;
- controlled red and green accents;
- white-blue text and gold borders.

Commander ThumB styling remains scoped to `.commander-page` or Commander-specific components so Palace and the card-game pages retain their own identity.

## Responsive behavior

- Hero imagery switches to the 960-pixel source on small screens.
- Story, system-map, and commander layouts collapse to one column.
- Civilization cards stack.
- Feature cards move from four columns to two and then one.
- Boss milestones move from five columns to two and then one.
- The fictional map keeps readable labels and removes no essential content.
- Four-game directories use four, two, and one columns.
- Mobile navigation retains all primary links and keyboard control.

Verified widths: 320, 375, 430, 768 portrait, 1024 landscape, 1366 laptop, and 1920 desktop.

## Accessibility

- One page-level H1 and semantic section headings.
- Keyboard-operable shared navigation and native links/buttons.
- Visible cyan focus indicators and 44-pixel minimum actions.
- Meaningful alt text on game art and empty alt text only on the redundant logo treatment.
- Screen-reader description on the stylized system map.
- Conflict direction is expressed in text, not color alone.
- Reduced-motion rules remove interface transitions and fixed-background effects.
- The page contains no flashing effects, autoplay, audio, cookies, analytics, or tracking.

## Metadata

- Title: `Commander ThumB | Four of Hearts Interactive`
- Description accurately states the upcoming original arcade adventure.
- Canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card, alternate-language, CSP, and local-asset metadata come from the shared page shell.
- `assets/og-commander-thumb.png` supplies the social preview.
- `VideoGame` JSON-LD describes an upcoming game without claiming public availability.
- The route is included in `sitemap.xml`.

## Performance

- The hero is served as a 155 KB 1600-pixel WebP with a 77 KB 960-pixel phone source and is intentionally eager-loaded as the primary visual.
- The directory tile has 35 KB and 15 KB responsive WebP sources.
- Below-the-fold illustrations and boss art are lazy-loaded, explicitly sized SVGs.
- The page adds no framework, animation library, remote font, analytics request, cookie, or storage dependency.

## Palace readability repair

Problems identified:

- layered translucent blues and haze weakened foreground separation;
- important labels depended on pale text over a detailed table;
- feedback and disabled states could look muted at ordinary phone brightness.

Repairs in `assets/commander-thumb.css`:

- solid royal-blue page and table surfaces;
- nearly opaque tutorial chrome;
- bright labels with dark text shadow;
- high-contrast cream feedback surface;
- explicit positive-feedback and disabled-control colors;
- stronger gold borders and focus treatment;
- preserved blue table atmosphere and artwork without placing key instructions on haze.

Palace rules, tutorial sequence, card behavior, and content were not changed.
