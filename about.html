Meet the founder · Player, teacher, observer

Games have rules. People make them interesting.

For more than fifty years, the founder has played, taught, tested, argued about, laughed over, and tried to understand games. More than twenty of those years included examining strategy and human interaction in a university environment.

The question always returns to the table: why does this game make people stay? He is a person who has spent a lifetime watching what happens when rules meet people.

That curiosity shapes Four of Hearts: clear enough to welcome a newcomer, deep enough to reward a regular, and human enough to create a story.
