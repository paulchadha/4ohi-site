Designing the Alien Invasion of Commander Thum-B

How purple silhouettes, readable formations, distinct enemy classes, and a massive Mothership create family-friendly arcade pressure.

A fleet you can read at full speed

The alien identity begins with dark purple armor, magenta energy, coral highlights, and silhouettes that remain distinct in motion. Stingrays dart through gaps, Voidrippers pressure the edges, Hexblades hold dangerous lanes, and Skitterers, Wraiths, and Bloodbites change the shape of a wave.

Formations communicate risk before they fire. That visual clarity lets the screen become spectacular without turning the game into noise.

The Mothership changes the scale

Boss encounters bring the Mothership close enough to dominate the sky. Its glowing core, armored wings, and supporting fleet make the threat feel enormous while the Blueguard remains precise and hopeful at the center of the defense.

The excitement stays family-friendly: bright projectiles, shields, energy bursts, and mechanical alien craft create danger without graphic violence.

Availability

Commander Thum-B is in development with no public build or announced release date. This article documents the current creative direction.
